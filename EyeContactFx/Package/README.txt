Eye Contact - Final Cut Pro plug-in (Apple Silicon)

1. Quit Final Cut Pro.
2. Double-click install.command. If macOS refuses to open it, run in Terminal:
     zsh /path/to/EyeContact/install.command
3. Restart Final Cut Pro. The effect is listed under Effects > Eye Contact.
   (The installer also copies the Motion template into ~/Movies/Motion Templates;
   Final Cut Pro does not show the effect without it.)

If FCP doesn't list it: pluginkit -m -v -p FxPlug
